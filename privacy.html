Trucker Find Privacy Policy

Effective September 30, 2026

This policy describes how Trucker Find handles information in its Android app and website. For privacy questions, contact the Trucker Find developer at littlefishdevolpment@gmail.com.

Information you provide

  • Account information: If you create an account, we use your email address, password authentication information, account identifier, and any driver name you provide to sign you in and synchronize your data. Supabase handles password authentication. Do not send your password to support.
  • Saved Areas: We store the place names, addresses or location descriptions, categories, and creation information you save, associated with your account. Avoid entering sensitive personal information in place names or notes.
  • Profile photo: If you choose to upload a photo, it is stored with your account in Supabase Storage. The app accesses the file you select, rather than uploading your whole photo library.
  • Support messages: If you email us, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send so we can respond.

Location, searches, and Maps

When you choose Nearby and grant location permission, the app requests your current location and passes its coordinates to Google Maps to display that location. Search terms and destinations you choose are also sent to Google Maps when you open a search or directions. Google Maps may use its own location permissions and settings.

Trucker Find does not maintain a continuous GPS travel history or continuously track your location in the background. Locations you deliberately save are retained as Saved Areas. You can refuse or revoke the app’s location permission and still use typed searches and saved destinations.

Voice controls

Voice recognition starts when you tap the microphone. The app launches the speech-recognition service installed on your device, which captures and processes your speech under that provider’s settings and privacy policy. Depending on the service and settings, audio may be processed online.

Trucker Find receives recognized text to match a quick search or Saved Area. It does not run an always-listening microphone or store audio recordings in your Trucker Find account. Search terms or a selected saved destination are passed to Maps when you proceed.

Floating icons and device storage

The optional “display over other apps” permission lets Trucker Find show movable quick-access icons while another app is open. An Android foreground service keeps these controls available and creates an ongoing notification. The floating controls do not capture other apps’ screen content or read text you type in them. You can close the controls or revoke overlay permission in Android settings.

The app and browser store sign-in session information, cached website files, settings, and an app installation identifier locally. Clearing app or browser storage removes local information and may sign you out; it does not delete your cloud account or Saved Areas. Android backup behavior is controlled by your device settings.

Service providers and technical information

Trucker Find uses Supabase for authentication, account data, photos, and account-deletion processing; GitHub Pages to host the website; and jsDelivr to deliver the web authentication library. The Android app uses Median’s app framework. Network services can receive technical information such as IP address, request time, browser or app details, and error or security information while serving requests. The Android framework also maintains installation and device information used by app functionality.

Google Maps and your device’s speech-recognition service handle the information you send to them under their own policies. Support email is handled through Gmail. These providers may process information outside your country. Relevant policies include Supabase, GitHub, jsDelivr, and Google.

We use account and saved-place information to provide the features you request, synchronize your data, respond to support requests, and protect the service. We do not sell this information or use it for targeted advertising. We may disclose information when required by law or necessary to address fraud, abuse, or security incidents.

Retention and deletion

Account information, Saved Areas, and profile photos remain in the live service until you remove them or delete your account. You can remove individual Saved Areas in the app.

To delete your account, open Profile → Delete account, enter your password, type DELETE, and confirm. Successful deletion removes your login account, driver profile, Saved Areas, and profile photo from the live service. Deletion cannot be undone. If an error occurs, some data may already be removed; retry or contact support.

You can also use the website without installing the app. Visit our account-deletion page for instructions and a support contact if you cannot sign in. Routine service logs and any existing backups expire according to the hosting providers’ retention settings; deletion does not erase records independently held by Google Maps, speech-recognition providers, or your email provider. Support correspondence is retained as needed to resolve requests and meet legal or security obligations.

Your choices and security

You may use quick searches without an account. A sign-in is needed to synchronize a profile and Saved Areas. A profile name, profile photo, location permission, and voice controls are optional. You can manage permissions in Android settings and request access, correction, or deletion through the support address above. Rights and available remedies depend on your location.

The app uses HTTPS for its web and backend connections and account-based controls for stored user data. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. Keep your password private and sign out on shared devices.

Children

Trucker Find is designed for truck drivers and is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can investigate and remove it where appropriate.

Policy changes

We will update this page and its effective date when our practices change. Material changes may also be communicated within the app.
