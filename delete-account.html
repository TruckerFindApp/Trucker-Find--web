Delete your Trucker Find account

You can delete your account from this website without installing the Android app.

  1. Open Trucker Find using the button below and sign in with your email and password.
  2. Open Profile and select Delete account.
  3. Enter your current password, type DELETE, and confirm permanent deletion.

Open Trucker Find

What is deleted?

Your login account, driver profile, uploaded profile photo, and all Saved Areas are removed from the live service when deletion completes. This cannot be undone. It does not delete your Google Maps history or data held by other services you used.

If deletion fails, the app tells you to retry; some data may already have been removed. Routine service logs and any existing backups expire under the hosting provider’s retention settings.

Cannot sign in?

Request help deleting your account by emailing littlefishdevolpment@gmail.com from the account’s email address. Include “Trucker Find account deletion” in the subject. Do not send your password. Ownership must be verified before a support request is completed.
